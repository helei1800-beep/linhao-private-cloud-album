# Linhao Private Cloud Album

**Back up your phone photos to the hard drive of your own home computer.** No cloud storage of ours, no monthly subscription for space, no ads.

- Website (Chinese): https://www.linhaotech.cn
- FAQ (Chinese, 39 Q&A): https://linhaotech.cn/faq.html
- Windows: search "**邻好**" in the Microsoft Store
- Android: search "**私有云相册**" in Chinese app stores (Tencent MyApp, Huawei, vivo, Baidu, Honor, ...)

> iOS and macOS are not supported yet.

## What it is

Install the app on your phone and the desktop app on your computer, pair them by scanning a QR code, and photos/videos taken on the phone are automatically synced to the hard drive of your home computer.

Pairing and authentication go through our server, but the server only **connects** the phone and the computer — the photo and video files themselves are stored on your own computer's hard drive, never uploaded to or kept by the server.

## What it is not

- **Not a cloud drive**: we do not sell storage and do not keep your photos. You own the disk, so there is no storage subscription.
- **Not a NAS**: no dedicated hardware required. A always-on home PC plus a hard drive is enough.
- **Not offline-capable**: pairing and authentication require an internet connection.
- **Not fully unattended**: it needs an always-on computer; when the PC is off, new photos wait on the phone and are uploaded automatically once the phone is back on the same network.

## Pricing and ads

Free to use. **No splash ads, no pop-ups, no renewal nagging.** We don't sell storage, so there is no "your space is full, please pay" prompt.
